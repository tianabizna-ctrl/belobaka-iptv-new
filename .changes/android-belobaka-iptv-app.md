BELOBAKA IPTV now installs on Android and can re-encode a stream on the device
with FFmpeg, from 1920×1080 down to 256×144 (including 1024×576, 704×576,
960×540 and 854×480). Pick a resolution in the player's local-transcoding
panel.

Inside the Android shell, Xtream portals go straight from the app.
